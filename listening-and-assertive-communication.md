### 1: What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

 - Focus completely on the speaker
 - Avoid distractions
 - Do not interrupt
 - Use appropriate body language
 - Encourage the speaker
 - Ask for clarification



### 2: According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

- Listen carefully without distractions.
- Try to understand the other person's point of view.
- Pay attention to their feelings and body language.
- Repeat the main point in my own words.
- Check if I understood them correctly.




### 3: What are the obstacles in your listening process?

- Phone and other distractions.
- Thinking about my reply while listening.
- Interrupting the speaker.
- Making assumptions too quickly.
- Stress, anger or lack of interest.




### 4: What can you do to improve your listening?

- Keep my phone away during important conversations.
- Focus fully on the speaker.
- Avoid interrupting.
- Ask questions when something is unclear.
- Take notes and confirm important points.


### 5: When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?

- When I want to avoid an argument.
- When I don't want to hurt someone's feelings.
- When I am not confident about my opinion.
- When I am talking to someone senior.
- Sometimes I agree even when I actually disagree.



### 6: When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

- When I am very angry or frustrated.
- When someone keeps interrupting me.
- When I am under a lot of pressure.
- When I feel that someone is not listening to me.



### 7: When do you switch to Passive-Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

- When I am angry but don't want to say it directly.
- I may use sarcasm or taunts.
- I may give someone the silent treatment.
- may say "Fine, whatever" when I am actually upset.
- Sometimes I may complain about someone instead of talking to them.



### 8: How can you make your communication assertive?

- Speak clearly and calmly.
- Say what I really think and feel.
- Use "I" statements instead of blaming others.
- Listen and respect the other person's opinion.
- Say no politely when I don't agree or cannot help.
