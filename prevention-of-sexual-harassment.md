## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

- Unwanted touching.
- Sexual jokes or comments.
- Sending inappropriate messages or pictures.
- Making someone feel uncomfortable.
- Not respecting personal boundaries.


## 2. What would you do if you face such behaviour?

- I would clearly tell the person to stop, if I feel it is safe.
- I would keep any messages or other proof.
- I would report it to HR/POSH Committee.
- I would not keep it to myself or ignore it.

## 3. What would you do if you witness such behaviour?

- I would not ignore it.
- I would support the person who faced it.
- I would report what I saw to the proper team.
- I would not gossip about the incident.

## 4. How should we behave appropriately at work?

- Respect everyone and their boundaries.
- Keep communication professional.
- Avoid inappropriate jokes, comments or gestures.
- Think about how our behaviour can make others feel.

## 5. How to handle cases of harassment?

- Take the complaint seriously.
- Listen to the person without judging them.
- Report it to the POSH Committee/HR.
- Keep the matter confidential.
- Follow the proper process to resolve the issue.
