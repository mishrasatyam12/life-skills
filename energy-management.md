# Energy Management

## 1: What are the activities you do that make you relax - Calm quadrant?

- Listening to music or watching movies helps me relax.
- Going for a walk or doing my hobbies makes me feel calm.
- Spending time with family and friends helps me feel relaxed.
- Meditation helps me clear my mind.

## 2: When do you find getting into the Stress quadrant?

- I feel stressed when I have many tasks or deadlines.
- Difficult topics and learning something new can make me stressed.
- Overthinking and unexpected problems also increase my stress.

## 3: How do you understand if you are in the Excitement quadrant?

- I feel energetic and motivated.
- I become curious and interested in the activity.
- I enjoy the task and stay highly engaged.
- I feel excited to learn or try something new.

## 4: Paraphrase the Sleep is your Superpower video in your own words in brief.

- Good sleep improves memory, focus and learning.
- It helps the brain and body recover.
- Lack of sleep affects mood and decision-making.
- Proper sleep improves overall health and performance.

## 5: What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?

- Follow a fixed sleep and wake-up time.
- Avoid screens before sleeping.
- Exercise regularly during the day.
- Avoid caffeine late in the evening.
- Keep the sleeping environment comfortable.

## 6: Paraphrase the video - Brain Changing Benefits of Exercise.

- Exercise improves memory and learning.
- It improves focus and concentration.
- It reduces stress and improves mood.
- Exercise helps create new brain connections.
- Regular exercise supports long-term brain health.

## 7: What are some steps you can take to exercise more?

- Set a fixed time for exercise every day.
- Start with simple exercises and increase slowly.
- Take short walks during breaks.
- Play sports that I enjoy.
- Exercise with friends to stay consistent.
