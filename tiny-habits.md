### 1. **In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?**

The most interesting idea for me was that even a very small habit can make a big difference when we do it consistently. Starting small makes it easier to continue.



### 2. **How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P.**

B = MAP means **Behavior = Motivation + Ability + Prompt**.

- **M – Motivation:** How much I want to do it.
- **A – Ability:** How easy it is to do.
- **P – Prompt:** A reminder or trigger.

I can make a habit easier by starting with a small action, making it simple, and using a clear prompt.

### 3. **Why is it important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of a habit?**

Celebrating gives the brain a positive feeling after completing the habit. This makes me want to repeat it and helps turn the behavior into a habit.

### 5. **What is the book's perspective about Identity?**

The book says habits are connected to our identity. Instead of only focusing on what I want to achieve, I should focus on the person I want to become.

For example, instead of saying **"I want to code daily,"** I can think **"I am a person who codes daily."**

### 6. **Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?**

I should make the habit simple and reduce the effort needed to start. I can prepare things beforehand and make the habit part of my daily routine.

### 7. **Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?**

I can add more effort or steps before doing the habit. For example, if I want to reduce phone usage, I can keep my phone away from my desk.



### 8. **Pick one habit that you would like to do more of? What are the steps that you can take to make the cue obvious or the habit more attractive or easy and/or response satisfying?**

A habit I want to do more is **coding every day**.

I can keep my laptop ready, set a fixed time for coding, start with a small task, and celebrate after completing it. This makes the habit easier and more satisfying.

### 9. **Pick one habit that you would like to eliminate or do less of? What are the steps that you can take to make the cue invisible or the process unattractive or hard or the response unsatisfying?**

A habit I want to reduce is **unnecessary phone scrolling**.

I can turn off notifications, keep my phone away while studying, and make distracting apps harder to access. This makes the habit less obvious and harder to do.
