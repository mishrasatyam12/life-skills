# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.

Grit means staying focused on a goal even when things get difficult. It is about being consistent, patient, and not giving up easily.

## 2. Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.

A growth mindset means believing that we can improve our skills through practice, effort, and learning. Mistakes are not failures, they are chances to improve.

## 3. What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

It means believing that our actions and decisions have a strong impact on our results. Instead of blaming others or situations, we focus on what we can control.

## 4. What are the key points mentioned by speaker to build growth mindset (explanation not needed).

- Accept challenges instead of avoiding them.
- Learn from mistakes.
- Take feedback positively.
- Practice consistently.
- Focus on improvement rather than perfection.
- Learn from people who are better than you.

## 5. What are your ideas to take action and build Growth Mindset?

I will take on difficult tasks instead of avoiding them. When I make mistakes, I will understand what went wrong and try again. I will take feedback seriously and keep improving my skills through regular practice.
