# learning-process

### 1:  What is the Feynman Technique?

   The Feynman Technique is explaining a concept in simple words to know how well you understand it.


### 2:  What was the most interesting story or idea for you?

- The most interesting idea for me was **focused and diffuse thinking**.
- Understanding with practice and repetition
- The best technique to learn is active recall.


### 3:  What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

- **Active thinking** is when we focus completely on a problem.
- **Diffuse thinking** is when we take a break and let our mind relax which helps us find new ideas and solutions.


### 4:  According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

- Choose what to learn.
- Break it into smaller parts.
- Learn enough to practice.
- Remove distractions.
- Practice for 20 hours.

### 5:  What are some actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

- Stay focused and avoid distractions.
- Understand concepts instead of memorizing them.
- Explain concepts in my own words.
- Practice by writing code.
- Teach others to test my understanding.
- Take breaks when I get stuck.
- Track my time and improve consistently.
