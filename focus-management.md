# Focus Management

## 1: What is Deep Work?

Deep work means doing one important task with full focus and without distractions.



## 2: How to do Deep Work properly?

- Choose one task.
- Keep the phone away.
- Avoid distractions.
- Work for a fixed time.
- Take breaks.
- Practice it regularly.


## 3: How can you use it in daily life?

- Keep my phone away while working.
- Do one thing at a time.
- Give some fixed time for focused work.
- Take breaks.
- Spend some time without phone or social media.



## 4: What are the dangers of social media?

- Wastes time.
- Reduces focus.
- Makes us check the phone often.
- Makes other work feel boring.
- Causes comparison with others.
- Disturbs our peace of mind.
